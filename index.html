Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Five Mins Push is a patented IV vitamin formulation delivering a clinically designed vitamin B complex and immune-support blend directly into the bloodstream in just 5–8 minutes — compared to the 45–60 minutes required by traditional IV therapies.

Five Mins Push must be administered by a licensed Medical Doctor (MD) or, at minimum, a certified Nurse Practitioner (NP). This is a clinical-grade product designed for professional medical settings — not for self-administration.

Clients typically experience sustained energy and immune benefits for 6–9 days per treatment, though individual results may vary based on health status, metabolism, and lifestyle factors.

Known side effects are mild and transient: a taste of vitamins during administration, a sensation of warmth throughout the body, and a possible urge to urinate shortly after treatment. These are consistent with standard IV vitamin therapy and resolve quickly.

We recommend once or twice monthly depending on the client's health goals and the practitioner's clinical judgment. With a 6–9 day effect window, monthly treatments provide consistent wellness support.

Yes. All ingredients in Five Mins Push are FDA-accepted for IV use. The product is compounded through FDA-compliant facilities, including Makers Nutrition (two FDA-approved NY locations) with raw materials sourced via Millipore/Sigma Aldrich.
